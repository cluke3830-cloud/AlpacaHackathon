# Alpaca Hackathon — Feasibility Probe, 2026-08-25

Question TC asked: *"Signal itself is great, but can we use it for options trading
and still make steady Sharpe and a statistically stable agent through Alpaca?"*

Answer: **the Alpaca stack is fully capable; the edge is not there.** SPY
defined-risk options price out as approximately fairly priced, and the MSAR/trend
gate does not significantly change that. Details and receipts below.

---

## 1. The Alpaca stack — empirically verified, not assumed

| fact | value | why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| paper auth | ACTIVE, $100k equity, $400k BP, **options level 3** | multi-leg spreads permitted |
| multi-leg orders | `OrderClass.MLEG` + `OptionLegRequest` | 4-leg condor is native, no legging |
| live chain | 13,316 SPY contracts; greeks+IV on 10,736 | Layer 3 gets delta/gamma/theta/vega + IV natively |
| live relative spread | median 2.6% of mid (p25 1.1 / p75 6.0) | cheap enough to trade |
| **option bar history starts** | **2024-01-18** | ~2.6 yrs; contains ZERO of the Constitution's 4 stress windows |
| **historical bid/ask** | **does not exist** (bars + trades only; quotes/snapshot/chain are live-only) | cost realism must be modelled, cf. bias #3 |
| **VIX** | **NOT on Alpaca** (`VIX` empty, `^VIX`/`$VIX` invalid) | MSAR's only input must come from FMP/FDR. VIXY/VXX/UVXY are decaying roll ETFs — using them would repeat the RV-for-IV anti-pattern |

## 2. Costs are NOT the obstacle — genuinely different from prior findings

Real SPY iron condor, 0.15-delta shorts, live quotes, entry slippage as % of credit:

| wing | 24 DTE | 36 DTE |
|---|---|---|
| $5 | 7.2% | 6.7% |
| $10 | 4.3% | 4.5% |
| $20 | 2.8% | 1.2% |

Compare the priors that killed earlier work: single-name ATM spreads were 1–4 vol
pts/leg (2–8 round trip, edge 5–15x below the cost wall) and the 0DTE straddle's
spread ≈ the entire premium. **SPY index options at 21–45 DTE are cheap.** The
cost wall that killed the single-name gamma-VRP book is not present here.

## 3. Two errors I made and caught — both worth recording

**(a) Flat-vol model artifact.** First expectancy test assumed one lognormal at
average IV and reported E = −0.54 "negative expectancy." Its own sanity check
failed (should have been ≈ −0.08 = the spread paid). Cause: SPY's smile is steep
(call IV 10.3% vs put IV 17.2%) and a flat-vol lognormal does not reproduce the
prices being traded. Same class as the gamma-VRP carry proxy. Discarded, replaced
with a model-free empirical-return test.

**(b) LOOK-AHEAD — the big one.** `condor_decompose.py` saved `dates[N:]`, the
window's **END** date. The gate was then evaluated on that date, i.e. it decided
whether to trade using data from the close of the very window it was trading.

| arm | gated E | Sh/trade | ann. Sharpe |
|---|---|---|---|
| end-dated (**the bug**) | +0.442 | +0.323 | **+1.24** |
| entry-dated (**correct**) | +0.061 | +0.027 | **+0.10** |
| entry-dated, +1 extra day lag | +0.063 | +0.028 | +0.11 |

Retained **8% of magnitude** → `causal_retest` classifies this as **COLLAPSE
(leak)**, not a fast-decaying real edge. The +1-day-lag arm confirms the corrected
version is clean. A Sharpe-1.24 strategy that was pure leakage.

## 4. Corrected results (vol-scaled credit, causal gate)

The static-credit flaw (charging today's VIX-15.4 credit to COVID's VIX-82 windows)
biased everything negative. Corrected by scaling BOTH strike distance and credit by
`k = VIX_entry / VIX_today`, holding the structure at constant delta.

| structure | ungated | gated | H1 | H2 | t (eff. n≈144) | worst |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| short put spread | +0.01 | +0.19 | +0.24 | +0.14 | +0.54 | −20.16 |
| iron condor | +0.02 | +0.52 | +0.74 | +0.29 | +1.46 | −18.03 |
| long call spread | +0.25 | +0.00 | +0.03 | −0.03 | +0.01 | −8.16 |

(annualized Sharpe; 2,459 windows 2016–2026)

**Findings:**
1. **Ungated premium selling E ≈ +0.007 to +0.027/share — fairly priced to three
   decimals.** Fifth independent confirmation in this fund (Korean skew, convexity
   test, gamma-VRP real instrument, now SPY verticals).
2. **Nothing clears significance.** Overlapping 17-day windows inflate n ~17x;
   effective independent n ≈ 144. Best t is +1.46, versus the standing pre-reg bar
   of |t| ≥ 2.5.
3. **MSAR contributes ≈ nothing here.** The vol-era veto blocks only 1.4% of days
   (it is tuned for the leveraged-ETF hold decision). The 200d MA does essentially
   all the work — even in the leaked version.
4. **The gate makes 2022 WORSE, not better** (put spread gated −3.83 vs ungated
   −1.36): it stands down through the bear but trades the bear *rallies*.
5. **The gate actively hurts the long structure** (+0.25 ungated → +0.00 gated) —
   it removes the recovery rallies where call spreads pay.

## 5. What this does and does not establish

**Does:** the Alpaca stack is capable and cheap; premium selling on SPY is fairly
priced; this signal+structure pairing has no validated edge.

**Does not:** prove no options edge exists. Real limitations remain —
- held-to-expiry only; no 50%-profit management, rolling, or stops (real premium
  sellers manage, which reshapes the distribution materially)
- gamma A+C leg omitted (needs the SPX chain parquet) — conservative, can only add
- one delta / one DTE / one underlying, deliberately unswept to avoid p-hacking
- snapshot method (today's structure vs historical paths), not a true backtest with
  historical option prices — Alpaca supports that only for 2024-01-18 onward

**Multiple-testing budget is spent for this session.** Cells tested: 3 structures
× {static, vol-scaled} × {ungated, gated, trend-only, vol-era-only} × 2 date
conventions. Any survivor found by further slicing would be memorization. A real
revisit needs fresh pre-registration.
